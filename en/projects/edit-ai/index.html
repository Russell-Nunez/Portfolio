Case study

Edit AI: AI video studio

Captions, cuts, editing, covers, translation and audio cleanup in one place, running on your PC.

Images

  • Captions

    Ready-made box, outline, shadow and neon styles, with a vertical preview.

  • Edit

    Smart cutting of silence and filler, with adjustable aggressiveness and transitions.

  • Create cover

    A 16:9 cover generated from a video scene, with a phrase and variations.

  • Clean audio

    Removes noise and normalizes volume on your PC, with adjustable strength.

  • Translate captions

    Translates SRT line by line, keeping each cue's timing.

Problem

Captioning and editing videos for social media takes several tools, subscriptions and time repeating the same steps.

Solution

A local pipeline with faster-whisper and FFmpeg, exposed by a FastAPI service with async jobs and history. Express mode uses Gemini to transcribe, correct, translate, suggest cuts and generate covers, with a Redis cache to avoid reprocessing and wasted credits. Captions are burned into the MP4 with ready-made styles. The front end is React, Vite and Tailwind, and everything starts with Docker Compose.

Impact
  • Burned-in captions with box, outline, shadow and neon styles
  • Automatic editing that cuts silence and filler, with transitions
  • Local processing, with optional cloud AI

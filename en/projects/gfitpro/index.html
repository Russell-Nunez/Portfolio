Case study

GFitPro: gym app for students and trainers

Mobile and web app with workouts, ranking, body assessments, nutrition and invoices, for two roles.

Images

  • Home

    Daily summary with rank, streak and shortcuts to what matters.

  • Workout

    Exercises with sets, load, estimated time and XP per session.

  • Ranking and XP

    Levels, achievements and position among students to keep motivation up.

  • Nutrition

    Calories, macros, hydration and logged meals, with a daily goal.

  • Body assessment

    Body composition, measurements and health, explained in plain language.

  • Compare assessments

    Pick two dates and see weight, fat and muscle evolution.

  • Invoices

    Plan, due date and payment history with status filters.

  • Files

    Plans, exams and progress photos organized by category.

  • Trainer: students

    Student list with search, filters and reassessment alerts.

  • Trainer: student

    Student profile, latest assessment and a shortcut to record a new one.

Problem

Gyms tend to spread workouts, body assessments, billing and communication across different tools, and students lose track of their own progress.

Solution

A React Native app with Expo that runs on iOS, Android and web. Students follow workouts, XP and ranking, assessments with date-to-date comparison, nutrition, invoices and files. Trainers manage students and record assessments. Authentication is a NestJS API with Prisma and SQLite. The UI follows a custom design system with tokens, condensed typography and animated components.

Impact
  • Complete student and trainer flows, including new assessment and comparison
  • Custom design system applied to every screen
  • Real login against a NestJS API; the rest of the data is still mocked
